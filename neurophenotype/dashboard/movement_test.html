What we're measuring

Stereotypy Score — Midline crossing rate. Rett (MECP2) patients show continuous midline hand-wringing, present in 100% of cases (Temudo et al. 2007).

Tremor Frequency — Dominant tremor frequency from wrist IMU. Pathological 3–8 Hz band.

Movement Irregularity — Sample entropy of hand trajectory. Low = repetitive stereotypy (Rett). High = jerky/excitable (Angelman).

Limb Rhythm Index — Periodicity of upper limb motion. High rhythm + low irregularity = Rett pattern.

IMU Acceleration (x/y/z) — Wrist accelerometer variability from Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense (if connected).
